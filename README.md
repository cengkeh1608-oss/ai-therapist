# Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis"
(u-motasimfuad-your-ai-therapist-s-diagnosis-4be14a41).

therapist hands its human — to `/output/entry.txt` (1–200 characters).

The line: "Session note: patient meditates daily. Ten minutes of
breathing, fifty minutes of wondering if he is breathing correctly."
